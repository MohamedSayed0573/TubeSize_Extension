# Embedded and generic video detection

Embedded and generic video detection inspects non-platform websites on demand when the user opens the toolbar popup, finding embedded YouTube, Twitch, or Kick iframes as well as generic HLS, DASH, and direct HTML5 videos across frames and Shadow DOMs to show per-quality size estimates or prompt playback when multiple paused videos are present.

## Sub-features

- `detect-embedded-platform` detects embedded YouTube (`youtube.com/embed/*`, `youtube-nocookie.com/embed/*`), Twitch (`player.twitch.tv`), and Kick (`player.kick.com`) `<iframe>` players on non-platform tabs, renders full platform size estimates with the active `Current` quality badge, and caches results under `local:<platform>:<id>`.
- `detect-generic-hls` pairs MSE `blob:` `<video>` players with `webRequest`-observed `.m3u8` master and variant playlists (grouping related variants under the master) or direct `.m3u8` sources to show VOD sizes or live rates without caching under `mediaCacheKeys`.
- `detect-generic-dash` traverses open and closed Shadow DOM trees up to depth 4 and parses `.mpd` XML manifests with separate audio tracks, folding audio into sizes by default and exposing codec, frame rate, standalone `Audio` row without `itag`, and `Include audio in sizes` in Advanced View.
- `detect-generic-direct` probes direct HTML5 `<video>` files via `HEAD` `Content-Length` (falling back to `Range: bytes=0-0` `Content-Range`) and renders the `Video` row label when intrinsic `videoHeight` is unavailable.
- `detect-multi-video-prompt` renders `Multiple videos detected` (`data-popup-state="multiple-idle"`) when two or more paused videos exist on a non-platform page, then switches to the active video once the user starts playing one and reopens the popup.
- `detect-multi-video-rtl` renders the multi-video prompt in Arabic RTL (`تم اكتشاف عدة فيديوهات`) with `dir=rtl`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open any non-platform website that embeds a YouTube, Twitch, or Kick player or plays an HLS, DASH, or direct HTML5 video, then click the TubeSize toolbar icon (or press `Alt+P`).
- On a non-platform page with two or more paused videos, start playing one video and reopen the toolbar popup to see its estimate.
- Turn on `Enable advanced view` in Settings to inspect DASH codecs, frame rates, the standalone `Audio` row, and the `Include audio in sizes` switch.

## Gotchas

- Wait for fixture readiness text (`Embed ready`, `HLS manifests loaded`, `DASH manifest loaded`, `Second Clip playing`) before calling `bun run verify popup open`. `TabMediaRegistry` records manifests from completed `webRequest` events, and `detectTabVideo` checks `video.readyState > 2` to mark a video as playing.
- Generic HLS, DASH, and direct estimates use `platform: "generic"` and do not write to `mediaCacheKeys`, whereas `platform-embed` estimates reuse the platform cache (`youtube:*`, `twitch:*`, `kick:*`).
- Reset `Enable advanced view` and `Include audio in sizes` after checking DASH Advanced View so later Simple View checks observe default settings.
- Live third-party sites can serve DRM-encrypted manifests, short-lived signed URLs, or servers that block `HEAD` and `Range` probes; in those cases TubeSize renders an inline error card with a retry button rather than failing silently.
